## Recommended Public Story

Win Empire LLC designs, builds, maintains, and secures the VPN software, deployment automation, website, and infrastructure tooling. Win's VPN Inc. operates the nonprofit public-benefit VPN service and either licenses, rents, or purchases the technology from Win Empire LLC under a written agreement approved by the nonprofit board.

Because the founder controls or owns Win Empire LLC, the nonprofit must treat this as a related-party transaction. The transaction can still be defensible, but only if it is documented cleanly:

- Written vendor/license/purchase agreement between Win Empire LLC and Win's VPN Inc.
- Board conflict-of-interest disclosure by the interested director/officer.
- Interested person recusal from board vote.
- Board minutes showing approval by disinterested voting directors.
- Fair-market-value evidence such as comparable software, hosting, cybersecurity, VPN, managed infrastructure, and engineering pricing.
- Clear statement that Win Empire LLC does not control donor funds.
- Clear ownership/license boundaries for source code, trademarks, infrastructure automation, and support obligations.

## Immediate Readiness Gaps

1. Board size: BBB Standard 2 expects at least five voting board members. The current public leadership list has four people. Add a fifth independent voting director before applying.
2. Related-party controls: the Win Empire LLC arrangement must be approved and documented by disinterested board members.
3. Board meetings: schedule at least three evenly spaced full-board meetings per year with majority attendance. Keep agendas, minutes, attendance, and voting records.
4. Treasurer/finance oversight: appoint a voting board member to oversee finances. The chair and treasurer should not be the same person.
5. CEO/founder review: create a board process to review the CEO/founder at least every two years.
6. Effectiveness policy: adopt a board-approved performance/effectiveness review policy.
7. Effectiveness report: create a written effectiveness report for board approval at least every two years.
8. Financial reporting: prepare GAAP-style annual financial statements, expense allocation, and budget. If gross income exceeds $1 million, plan for an audit by a CPA.
9. Donor privacy: website and donation flows need a clear privacy policy and a way for donors to opt out of external name/address sharing.
10. Website disclosures: publish mission, program accomplishments, officer/director roster, mailing address, most recent Form 990 when available, audited financials if applicable, and annual report.

## Draft Application Answer Bank

### Mission

Win's VPN Inc. provides privacy, cybersecurity, malicious-site filtering, and anti-censorship VPN access to help people reach uncensored news, education, and safer communications. The organization prioritizes underserved and high-risk users who need reliable access to the open internet.

### Programs

1. Low-cost VPN access with Verified No Log policy boundaries.
2. Free limited-speed server access for users who cannot pay.
3. Malicious website, tracker, ad, and unsafe-file filtering through aggregated threat intelligence.
4. Anti-censorship routing through Winn Protocol®.
5. Planned local nonprofit cybersecurity audit services beginning in 2027, depending on donations received.

### Technology And Vendor Relationship

Win Empire LLC designs and maintains the software and infrastructure tooling used by Win's VPN Inc. Win's VPN Inc. operates the public service and uses the Win Empire LLC technology under a written board-approved related-party agreement. The arrangement is used for trust, safety, continuity, and operational security reasons because the founder can directly verify the builder's integrity. Disinterested directors should approve the arrangement after reviewing conflict disclosures and fair-market-value evidence.

### Donor-Facing Statement

Donations help expand free and low-cost access, add server locations, improve cybersecurity filtering, and support future nonprofit cybersecurity audit services. Donors may leave location-priority comments in donation notes so the board can consider demand when planning expansion.

### Risk Controls

Win's VPN Inc. uses written legal acceptance, a verified no-log boundary, intrusion detection/prevention monitoring, weekly maintenance, backups, documented change management, quality control, IP/open-source license compliance, incident response, and vendor continuity controls.
