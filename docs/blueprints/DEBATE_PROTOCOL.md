# Permanent Presidential Debate Protocol

To prevent blind spots, hallucinated configurations, and single-model cognitive bias, the following Permanent Presidential Debate Protocol is codified as the mandatory standard across all IDEs, audit rounds, and systems engineering decisions:

## Debate Protocol Workflow

```
Round 1: Independent Plan Declaration
(Antigravity, Sol Pro, Opus 5)
        │
        ▼
Round 2: Adversarial Cross-Examination
(Pairwise Difference Analysis & Proofs)
        │
        ▼
Round 3: Unfinished Item Resolution
(Mathematical Reconciliation & Treaty)
        │
        ▼
   100% Unanimous Agreement?
        ├────────────┐
       Yes           No
        │            │
        │            ▼
        │    Future Rounds (Round 4+)
        │    until Total Consensus
        ▼
Production Release: Code, Configs, Implementations
```

## Protocol Rounds

1. **Round 1 (Independent Plan Declaration):**
   All 3 candidates (Antigravity, Sol Pro, Opus 5) independently state their complete, uncompromised technical plan for every architecture, parameter, throughput ceiling, memory footprint, kernel choice, and engineering method. No peer text is shown during Round 1. Every numeric claim must be grounded with mathematical proofs or empirical measurements.

2. **Round 2 (Adversarial Cross-Examination & Comparative Analysis):**
   Candidates directly cross-examine rivals. Every candidate receives the verbatim declarations of the other two candidates. Every divergence in memory arithmetic, kernel choice, allocation churn, or numerical stability must be challenged with mathematical proofs and empirical rooflines. Each candidate identifies points of dispute and concedes validated facts.

3. **Round 3 (Reconciliation & Unanimous Treaty Ratification):**
   Unfinished disputes from Round 2 are resolved through formal compromises and mathematical proofs. No code, configuration, or protocol change is committed until all 3 candidates sign off unanimously with 100% agreement.

4. **Future Rounds (Round 4+) if Needed:**
   If consensus is not 100% unanimous at Round 3, further debate rounds continue until total consensus is reached.

## Mandatory Rules
- Zero em dashes and zero en dashes across all text, transcripts, and code.
- Grounded mathematics: all throughput targets and buffer sizes must satisfy empirical rooflines.
- Zero lazy placeholders or ellipsis comments.
