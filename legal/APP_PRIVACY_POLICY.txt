Verified No Log policy. Win's VPN does not collect or keep VPN activity logs. Win's VPN does not log browsing history, DNS query history, traffic contents, raw VPN traffic, or user destination activity. Limited service metadata may be stored to operate the service, including account status, payment/referral status, legal acceptance evidence, security/abuse-prevention records, privacy-preserving device-limit metadata, and free-access connection events. A free-access event records the shared free identity, device and source-IP buckets, device public-key fingerprint, platform and device model, selected gateway, stealth choice, a user-agent hash, and time. Device and account identifiers. After you accept this bundled Privacy Policy, the app generates a random app-install identifier on the device and sends it to Win's VPN with your account or free-access identity and device details (device model, platform, app version, locale, selected location, and capability flags). That identifier is created locally and is not derived from a device hardware identifier or an advertising identifier; on Android, Windows, and Linux it is kept in the app's private storage and reinstalling produces a new one, and on Apple platforms it is kept in the device keychain and can survive a reinstall on the same device. The app also generates a long-lived cryptographic key pair on first use of each account and sends the derived public key to Win's VPN with every account verification and configuration renewal request as a persistent identifier for connection provisioning, the three-device limit, and abuse prevention; the private key never leaves the device. Neither identifier is derived from hardware or advertising identifiers, used for advertising or tracking, sold, or shared for advertising. The account-side records are deleted or overwritten when the device is deactivated, replaced, or no longer needed.

Third-party connections. So the app can still reach a working gateway when the usual DNS or CDN paths are censored, it requests a small gateway-recovery file hosted on GitHub at raw.githubusercontent.com; GitHub necessarily receives the connection IP and ordinary request metadata for that request, the file only selects among gateways already built into the signed app, and no account number, device identifier, or other user data is sent with it. Only after you ask to show the approximate-location map, and before the VPN connects, the app asks first-party Win's VPN origins and gateways for an IP-derived map scene, then uses Cloudflare's speed.cloudflare.com/meta as the final fallback. Those services receive the pre-VPN connection IP and ordinary request metadata. Apple Maps receives the requested region and connection IP on Apple platforms. On other platforms, a first-party Win's VPN endpoint serves OpenStreetMap-derived tiles, so the upstream tile provider sees the Win's VPN server instead of your device. Win's VPN does not request GPS or retain returned IP, city, postal-code, or map-view history. While the app is disconnected, it may query api.ipify.org to display your current public IP address next to the connection status indicator; that service receives the connection IP and ordinary request metadata, no account or device data is sent, and the request is never made while a VPN tunnel is active. When you run the in-app speed test, the app measures throughput against speed.cloudflare.com and cachefly.cachefly.net for every region and, for nodes in the western United States, losangeles.ca.speedtest.frontier.com, lsanca-speedtest-ookla-02.st.charter.com, and speedtest.lax2.sonic.net; each receives the connection IP, ordinary request metadata, and the measurement traffic, with no account or device data included, and only while the test is running. Selected split-tunneling package identifiers stay in protected local settings and are passed locally to the operating-system VPN service; the full installed-app inventory is not uploaded.

Abuse prevention. To throttle repeated failed attempts and protect accounts, gateways, and infrastructure, the service keeps short-lived request counters and security-event records keyed to a hashed form of the source IP address, device identifier, or account number rather than to the value itself; those records exist only for security, fraud prevention, abuse response, and device-limit enforcement and never contain VPN traffic, browsing history, DNS queries, or destination activity.

Camouflage safety mode. Android and iOS launcher icon camouflage and Android/iOS in-app calculator camouflage are user-safety features for high-risk censorship environments, including Burma/Myanmar, where a visible VPN interface can expose users to retaliation. On Android and iOS both are applied automatically once a device is activated and cannot be switched off in the app; the real interface is shown after the PIN is entered. Android TV and desktop builds have neither. They do not hide malicious activity, iOS may display an operating-system confirmation when changing the icon, and the operating system's VPN status remains visible while a tunnel is active. On Android, Win's VPN uses Android VpnService as a core user-facing VPN function and does not use it to inject ads, monetize traffic, manipulate advertising, sell traffic data, or collect browsing history, DNS query history, traffic contents, or destination activity.
