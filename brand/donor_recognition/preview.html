Win’s VPN Inc. — Donor Recognition Artwork

Four tier badges and one certificate of recognition. Every asset on this page is an embedded, self-contained SVG: no webfonts, no remote images, no network requests of any kind. Badges are shown with the tax-year token substituted as 2026.

1 — Badges at 100×100 px, on white

The size that matters: digital credential platforms and email signatures render badges small. Each tier has its own silhouette, so the four are separable before you read a single word — bare shield, ring, wings, faceted octagon.

2 — Badges at 100×100 px, on dark

The plate is opaque with rounded corners and a metal keyline, so each badge keeps its own edge on a dark page as well as a light one.

3 — Badges at 64×64 px (stress test)

Below the design target. Tier words start to soften here, but silhouette and metal still separate all four. Do not issue below 64 px.

4 — Badges at 300×300 px

Large size: the star canton, stripe field, padlock keyhole, feather separation and facet edges resolve here, without adding noise at 100 px.

5 — Badges as shipped (unsubstituted)

The files on disk carry {{TAX_YEAR}}. Replace that token before issuing. The pill is sized for a four-digit year, so the raw token overflows here by design.

6 — Certificate of Recognition

Landscape US Letter, 11×8.5 in, print-ready at 100% scale. The left-hand panel is the contemporaneous written acknowledgment required by IRS Publication 1771 — a required element, not decoration. Switch between the shipped template and two worked examples, including the harder case where goods or services were provided. Example B is the exception, not the pattern: a recognition tier must never carry a benefit with a real market price, or every gift at that tier becomes a quid pro quo contribution.

7 — IRS Publication 1771 element check

Required contents of a contemporaneous written acknowledgment, and where each one sits on the certificate. Source: IRS Publication 1771 (Rev. 11-2023), page 3.

Required elementWhere it appears
1. Name of the organizationLetterhead wordmark, and the ORGANIZATION row of the acknowledgment panel
2. Amount of any monetary contributionAMOUNT OF CASH CONTRIBUTION row
3. Description (but not value) of any propertyNON-CASH CONTRIBUTION, IF ANY row, with a note that the organization does not state a value
4. Statement that no goods or services were provided, if soGoods-or-services statement block, lines 1–3
5. Description and good-faith estimate of value, if any were providedGOOD-FAITH VALUE row plus the statement block
6. Intangible religious benefits statement, if applicableDeliberately omitted — Win’s VPN Inc. is not organized for religious purposes, so this element can never apply
