Microsoft Store / Partner Center Export Compliance Facts To Review

answering Partner Center encryption and export questions for Win's VPN
1.0.17+34 (product 1.0.17 / four-part 1.0.17.34).

Facts from the current app:
- The app is a VPN client.
- The app uses encryption for VPN tunneling and secure network communication.
- Primary tunnel: Winn Protocol v3 on the local Windows datapath.
- Ordered Winn v1 compatibility path: AWG then Xray after a served Winn v3
  attempt. Direct AWG or Xray is not a separate request site.
- Local Windows engines include winn-datapath.exe, winsvpn_winn3_engine.exe,
  winsvpn_awg_engine.exe, amneziawg.exe, awg.exe, and xray.exe.
- The app is not a medical, military, or government-only app.
- The app is intended for general consumer/business privacy and network
  security use.
- There is no current 1.0.17+34 signed website or Partner Center installer.
  Unsigned local EXEs, development MSIX identities, and historical 1.0.14+29
  files cannot carry an encryption declaration or export-compliance receipt.
- Partner Center encryption, export, and IARC answers remain
  OWNER-BLOCKED: ABSENT until owner/legal review completes them in the
