Win's VPN is a user-initiated encrypted VPN for Android phones and tablets.

The tunnel uses Winn v3 as the product's primary protocol. Winn v1 AWG
fallback is the compatibility path after a served Winn v3 attempt. A served
Winn v3 lead attempt cannot be skipped. Direct AWG is not a separate request
site.

Tap the power control to start the VPN, approve Android's VpnService prompt,
and disconnect from the app or the persistent VPN notification at any time.
Split tunneling is the on-device picker named Exclude Apps From VPN (Split
Tunneling). Installed-app inventory stays on the device.

The Play listing name is Win's VPN. The installed application label is the
Calculator disguise. Calculator camouflage is phone-only.

Privacy policy: https://www.winsvpn.org/privacy/
