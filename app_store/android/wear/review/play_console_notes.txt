Wear OS Play Console notes for org.winsvpn.app 1.0.16+33 (watch versionCode 3301).

Form factor:
- Same application ID as the phone companion: org.winsvpn.app
- com.google.android.wearable.standalone=false
- Required hardware feature android.hardware.type.watch
- Wear listing/screenshot inventory: owner-blocked

What the watch does:
- Remote connect, disconnect, and country select for the paired phone
- Renders phone-reported tunnel status
- Never presents Android VPN consent; the user must approve VPN on the phone

What the watch does not do:
- No INTERNET permission
- No VpnService
- No QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES
- No account number, key, node address, or tunnel config on the watch
- No local Winn v3, AmneziaWG, or Xray native engines

Protocol (executed on the paired phone, not the watch):
- Primary: Winn v3
- Ordered Winn v1 compatibility path: AWG first, then Xray
- A served Winn v3 lead attempt cannot be skipped

Reviewer path:
1. Install the matching phone release of org.winsvpn.app on a paired phone.
2. Confirm the watch app appears only after the phone companion is installed.
3. Open the watch app (launcher name Calculator).
4. Use the watch toggle only after VPN consent has been granted on the phone.
