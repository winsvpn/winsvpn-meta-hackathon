Win's VPN on Wear OS is a non-standalone companion for the paired phone app.

The watch can start or stop the phone tunnel and choose a country. It does not
create a VPN on the watch, does not hold account numbers or keys, and does not
talk to VPN servers. Pairing uses the Wearable Data Layer only.

The paired phone runs Winn v3 as the product's primary protocol. Winn v1 AWG
then Xray fallback is the ordered compatibility path after a served Winn v3
attempt. Those native engines ship in the phone form factor of org.winsvpn.app,
not in this Wear package.

Play must not offer this watch app to unpaired watches. The watch launcher
label is the permanent Calculator disguise; the Play listing name remains
Win's VPN because this is the Wear form factor of the same application ID.

Privacy policy: https://www.winsvpn.org/privacy/
