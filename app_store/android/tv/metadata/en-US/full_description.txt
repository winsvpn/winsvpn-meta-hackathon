Win's VPN is a user-initiated encrypted VPN for Android TV.

This is the TV form factor of org.winsvpn.app, not a second package. Use a
D-pad / Leanback remote. Do not treat phone touch screenshots as TV evidence.

The tunnel uses Winn v3 as the product's primary protocol. Winn v1 AWG
fallback is the compatibility path after a served Winn v3 attempt. A served
Winn v3 lead attempt cannot be skipped.

The TV flavor inherits QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES and exposes the same per-app picker
named Exclude Apps From VPN (Split Tunneling). Calculator camouflage is
disabled on TV. The application label is Win's VPN.

Privacy policy: https://www.winsvpn.org/privacy/
