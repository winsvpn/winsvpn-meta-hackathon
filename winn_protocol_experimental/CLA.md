## Why this exists

Winn Protocol is released under the **GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 or later**. Win's VPN Inc. is a US 501(c)(3) non-profit and also
offers the software under a separate commercial licence to organisations that
cannot comply with the AGPL's network-use provision.

**Offering that commercial licence requires Win's VPN Inc. to hold the rights
to every part of the work it offers.** Without signed CLAs, the commercial
licence cannot lawfully be offered, and the funding it provides disappears.
This is the sole reason a CLA is required here.
